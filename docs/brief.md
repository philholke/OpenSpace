Gents, tomorrow, as the last Friday of the month, we’re running our first Mission Systems mini hackathon.

The objective is simple: take the day to experiment, tinker and build something interesting. This is not about producing something commercially useful or immediately ready for production. It is about testing new ideas, technologies and ways of building — whether that means a new model, a strange product idea, a different UI, a voice or visual experience, an internal tool, or something completely unrelated to our usual work.

Projects can be F&B or hospitality-related, but they do not need to be.
They may use AI, but they do not need to.

The only real requirement is that you build and ship something.



Before the hackathon
Please post what you intend to build in this channel no later than 9:00am SGT tomorrow. Ideally, post it tonight so everyone can see what is being explored.

Keep the initial proposal short:
• What are you building?
• Why is it interesting?
• What technology, model or idea are you experimenting with?

The scope can change during the day. This is simply to make everyone commit to a direction before building starts.

Timeline
9:00am — Hackathon begins. Final deadline for posting your proposed project.
5:00pm — Hard submission deadline.

I’ll review all submissions over the weekend (you are of course all welcome to check everyone's submission out too!)

On Monday morning we’ll hop on a call and everyone can briefly present what they cooked up, show the project working and share anything interesting they learned along the way.



Submission format
Your final submission should be a single main post in this channel, with any additional information, screenshots or updates placed inside its thread.

The main post should include:
• The name of the project
• A short explanation of the idea and what you built — approximately two or three short paragraphs
• The GitHub repository
• A live Vercel, Fly.io or web link, where applicable
• Screenshots, a short recording or usage instructions if the project cannot be easily opened and tested

A working prototype is more important than a polished presentation. Keep the scope small enough that you can actually ship something by 5:00pm.



Infrastructure
Each of you may use, under the Mission Systems accounts:
• Up to two Supabase databases
• Up to two Fly.io applications
• Vercel for frontend or web deployments

These costs will be covered by Mission Systems. For any other paid service, API or infrastructure that could create meaningful charges, check before using it. Within reason, I'm ok to subsidise some token costs on OpenRouter or otherwise. @Daniel David Chan can gatekeep.

Rules and guidelines
• Build something new during the hackathon. Existing libraries, templates and boilerplates are fine, but the actual project should be created tomorrow.
• Do not use customer production data or interfere with any live Mission Systems application.
• Do not commit credentials, tokens or secrets to GitHub.
• You may use any models, agents, frameworks or development tools.
• Control the scope. One complete, working idea is better than five unfinished features.
• Optimise for experimentation and learning, not commercial viability.
• Strange, ambitious and slightly unnecessary ideas are encouraged.

The goal is to create room for ideas we would not normally prioritise during a regular workday, and ideally discover a few things worth exploring further.

Happy hacking!
